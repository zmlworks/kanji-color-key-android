1. Kanji stroke data — KanjiVG
------------------------------
assets/pool.json (stroke paths, stroke counts and stroke order of 4096 kanji) is derived from
KanjiVG (https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/) release r20260714, Copyright (C) Ulrich Apel,
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0 Unported license
(CC BY-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Changes (made by the kanji-color-key project, https://github.com/zmlworks/kanji-color-key):
4096 kanji were selected (JIS X 0208 level 1 and part of level 2), coordinates were rounded to 0.1,
and metadata was removed. The file is byte-identical to data/pool.json of kanji-color-key v1.1.0.

pool.json, as a derivative of KanjiVG, is distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0 (not under the MIT license).
The kanji drawn on screen are rendered from this data.

2. Encoding scheme
------------------
The encoding (kanji pool order, colors, check code and surplus bits) is that of kanji-color-key
v1.1.0 by zmlworks (MIT), ported from JavaScript to Java.
