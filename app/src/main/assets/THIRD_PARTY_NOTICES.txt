1. Kanji and kana stroke data — KanjiVG
---------------------------------------
assets/pool.json (stroke paths, stroke counts and stroke order of 4096 kanji) and assets/kana.json
(the same for 32 kana: あおかきけさせたなにはふほまむもやを ウオキケサチツテネホミモヨヰ) are derived from
KanjiVG (https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/) release r20260714, Copyright (C) Ulrich Apel,
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0 Unported license
(CC BY-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Changes (made by the kanji-color-key project, https://github.com/zmlworks/kanji-color-key):
4096 kanji (JIS X 0208 level 1 and part of level 2) and 32 kana were selected, coordinates were
rounded to 0.1, and metadata was removed. The files are byte-identical to data/pool.json and
data/kana.json of kanji-color-key v1.2.0.

pool.json and kana.json, as derivatives of KanjiVG, are distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0
(not under the MIT license). The kanji and kana drawn on screen are rendered from this data.
